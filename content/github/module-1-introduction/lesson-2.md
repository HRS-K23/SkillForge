# Creating your account and profile

1. Go to [github.com](https://github.com) and choose **Sign up**.
2. Pick a username. It becomes part of every URL you share, so choose something professional.
3. Verify your email address.
4. Turn on **two-factor authentication** under *Settings → Password and authentication*.
5. Add a profile picture and a short bio under *Settings → Public profile*.

> Tip: your profile is often the first thing a recruiter or collaborator sees. Keep it tidy.
