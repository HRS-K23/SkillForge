# Layout grids and alignment

Consistent spacing makes a design look professional.

- Select a frame and add a **layout grid** in the properties panel (for example 12 columns).
- Use the alignment buttons to line up selected objects.
- Hold `Alt` (Option on Mac) while hovering to see the distance between objects.
- Stick to a spacing scale such as 8, 16, 24 and 32 pixels.
