# Working with frames

A **frame** is a container that represents a screen or a section of a screen. Think of it as an artboard.

## Create a frame

1. Press `F`.
2. In the properties panel, choose a preset such as **iPhone** or **Desktop**.
3. Click and drag on the canvas, or click a preset to place it.

## Why frames matter

- Anything placed inside a frame moves with it.
- Frames can be nested (a card inside a page).
- Frames are the unit used for prototyping and exporting.
