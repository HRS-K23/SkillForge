# What is Notion?

Notion is an all-in-one workspace. You build **pages** out of **blocks**, and you can turn collections of pages into **databases**.

## Common uses

- Personal notes and to-do lists
- Team wikis and documentation
- Project and task tracking
- Reading lists, habit trackers and journals
