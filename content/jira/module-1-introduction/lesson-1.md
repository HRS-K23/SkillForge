# What is Jira?

Jira is a work-tracking tool from Atlassian, widely used by software teams to plan and track work.

## Core ideas

- **Project** - a collection of work for a team or product.
- **Issue** - a single piece of work, such as a task or a bug.
- **Board** - a visual view of issues moving through stages.
- **Sprint** - a short, fixed period (often two weeks) in which a team completes planned work.
