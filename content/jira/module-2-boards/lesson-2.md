# Planning a sprint

1. Open the **Backlog**, the ordered list of upcoming work.
2. Rank issues so the most important are at the top.
3. Estimate each story, commonly with story points.
4. Click **Create sprint**, drag issues into it and set a start and end date.
5. Click **Start sprint**. The board now shows only the sprint's work.

At the end of the sprint, choose **Complete sprint**. Unfinished issues move back to the backlog or into the next sprint.
