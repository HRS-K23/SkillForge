# Working with boards

A Scrum or Kanban board shows columns for each status. Drag an issue card between columns to change its status.

- Use **filters** and **quick filters** to show only your issues.
- **Assign** issues so ownership is clear.
- Use **labels** and **priority** to organise and sort work.
- Click a card to see its description, comments and activity history.
